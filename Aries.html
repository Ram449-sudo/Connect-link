About Aries

Aries, the first sign of the zodiac, is associated with those born between March 21 and April 19. These individuals are known for their boldness, ambition, and confidence. As natural leaders, Aries thrive in challenging environments, often charging ahead with determination and enthusiasm. Their energetic and passionate nature makes them stand out in any group or situation.

The mythology behind Aries traces back to the Greek tale of Phrixus and the Golden Fleece. In the story, a golden ram saved Phrixus and carried him to safety. As gratitude, the ram was sacrificed and placed in the sky by Zeus, becoming the Aries constellation we know today. This origin symbolizes protection, courage, and divine favor—traits deeply embedded in the Aries identity.
