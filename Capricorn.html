Capricorn

In astrology, Capricorn is the tenth sign of the zodiac, represented by the Sea-Goat, a mythical creature with the body of a goat and the tail of a fish. It symbolizes ambition, discipline, and perseverance. Ruled by Saturn, Capricorns are known for their hardworking nature, strong sense of responsibility, and long-term planning. The Capricorn period spans from approximately December 22 to January 19.

Mythological Origins: Capricorn's mythology traces back to the Sumerian god Enki, a wise and water-associated deity depicted as a goat-fish hybrid. In Greek mythology, it is linked to Pan, who transformed into a half-goat, half-fish to escape the monster Typhon, symbolizing adaptability and resilience.
