Libra is an air sign ruled by Venus, symbolizing balance, justice, and harmony. Its symbol, the Scales, represents fairness and equilibrium. Libra individuals are known for their charm, diplomacy, and strong sense of fairness. They seek harmony in relationships and are natural peacemakers. Ruled by Venus, they have a love for beauty, art, and romance.

Mythological Origins: Libra is linked to Themis, the Greek goddess of divine law and justice, and her daughter Astraea, the goddess of innocence. In mythology, Astraea left Earth due to human corruption, and her scales became the constellation Libra. It’s also connected to the scales of Dike, another goddess of justice.
