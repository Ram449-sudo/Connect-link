Pisces

In astrology, Pisces is the twelfth and final sign of the zodiac, representing the period from approximately February 19 to March 20, and is symbolized by two fish swimming in opposite directions.

Mythological Origins: The two fish are often associated with the Greek myth of Aphrodite and Eros, who transformed into fish to escape the monster Typhon. Alternatively, the two fish are said to have carried Aphrodite and Eros to safety.
