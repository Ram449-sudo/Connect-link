Taurus

In astrology,Taurus is the second sign of the zodiac, represented by the Bull, and includes people born between April 20 and May 20. It is an Earth sign ruled by Venus, the planet of love, beauty, and comfort. This makes Taurus individuals practical, patient, and deeply connected to the physical world—they enjoy luxury, good food, art, and nature. Known for their loyalty, stability, and determination, Taurus people are dependable and often resist change. They value routines, security, and long-term commitments. While they can be stubborn or slow to adapt, they are also loving, grounded, and reliable friends or partners.

Mythological Origins: In Greek mythology, Taurus is linked to the story of Zeus and Europa. Zeus, the king of the gods, fell in love with a beautiful princess named Europa. To get close to her, he transformed himself into a gentle and stunning white bull. Europa was drawn to the bull’s calm nature and climbed onto its back. Zeus then carried her across the sea to the island of Crete, where he revealed his true identity. To honor this moment, Zeus placed the image of the bull in the sky, creating the Taurus constellation.
